# Aethra logo brief (for a designer)

Status: the logo in use is a **provisional mark** drawn in code (`aethra-site/public/img/logo-mark.svg`, `favicon.svg`). This brief is for the final identity. Do not start before the name has cleared a BOIP/EUIPO trademark check (a company called AETHRA already exists in the same sector).

## Company in one paragraph
Aethra is a prototype-stage company developing CubeSats and AI that help vehicles switch to an eco mode in areas with high air pollution. B2B: municipalities, fleet operators, vehicle manufacturers, mobility platforms and investors. Voice: factual, calm, precise, open (see `docs/brand-guidelines.md`).

## What the data says (design skill, industry "Aerospace / Aviation")
| Topic | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| Recommended styles | Modern, abstract, dynamic, emblem |
| Colours | Blue and white (reference palette #1E3A8A, #0EA5E9, #FFFFFF). Aethra's navy `#0B1B33` and blue `#1F5FD1` sit in this family |
| Typography | Clean, modern, geometric sans |
| Typical symbols | Wing, arrow, globe, bird: avoid copying these clichés |
| Mood | Innovative, precise, reliable |
| Avoid | Cluttered, heavy, grounded |

The air-quality side of the company points towards calm and clean rather than "eco green"; a green leaf would also wrongly imply an environmental claim the company cannot yet prove.

## Directions to explore
1. **Orbit mark (current idea, refined):** a planet, an orbit that passes in front of it with a small gap, and a small square on the orbit for the CubeSat. Pros: ties to the product, reads at 16 px. Risk: close to many "planet and ring" marks; check distinctiveness.
2. **Monogram A:** a geometric A whose crossbar is an orbit line. Pros: ownable, works as an app/profile icon. Risk: more complex at small sizes.
3. **Wordmark only:** AETHRA in a custom geometric sans with one drawn detail (for example the A without a crossbar, or an orbit dot above the letters). Pros: calm, serious, easy to localise. Risk: needs strong letterform craft.

## Requirements
- Works at 16 px (favicon), 28 px (header) and large (deck, poster); keep stroke weights robust at small sizes.
- Versions: navy on light, white on navy, one-colour (black). No gradients required for recognition.
- Lock-ups: mark + wordmark horizontal; mark alone square. Clear space = one logo height; minimum mark width 24 px.
- Do not depict the prototype or imply performance (no "clean air" badges, no leaf, no percentages).
- No illustrations of vehicles or satellites with technical detail (the "how" is confidential).
- Deliver: SVG (outlined text), PNG, favicon set, and a one-page usage sheet.
- Accessibility: contrast of the mark against its backgrounds at least 3:1.

## Open decisions for the client
- Final name (and trademark outcome)
- Direction 1, 2 or 3 to pursue
- Whether a tagline is part of the lock-up (the website headline is "Cleaner air, right where traffic is heaviest")
